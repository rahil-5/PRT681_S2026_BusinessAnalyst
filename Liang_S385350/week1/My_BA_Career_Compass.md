# My BA Career Compass

Instead of a plain list of job postings, I mapped my research onto four
directions — what the role actually *is*, what it *pays/expects*, what
*skills* keep showing up, and what *red flags* to watch for.

## North: What the role actually is (in plain language)

A Business Analyst's real job, stripped of jargon: **find out what
people actually need, then make sure the thing that gets built matches
that need.** Everything else — diagrams, user stories, workshops — is
just tooling in service of that one sentence.

On LinkedIn, most BA profiles and job descriptions summarise the role
around a similar core set of responsibilities:
- Gathering and documenting business/functional requirements from
  stakeholders
- Analysing current processes and identifying gaps or inefficiencies
- Translating business needs into specifications developers/delivery
  teams can build from
- Supporting UAT (user acceptance testing) to confirm delivered work
  matches what was asked for
- Producing reports/dashboards (often in Excel or Power BI) to support
  decisions with data, not just opinion

Titles that map to this at entry level: Junior/Graduate BA, Business
Systems Analyst, Process Analyst, Product Analyst.

## East: Skills that kept showing up in postings / LinkedIn profiles

- Requirements gathering & documentation (BRDs, user stories)
- Stakeholder management and communication
- Process mapping (as-is/to-be, swimlane diagrams)
- SQL and Excel for basic data validation
- Power BI or similar (Tableau) for reporting/dashboards
- Agile/Scrum familiarity (backlog, sprint ceremonies)
- Tools: Jira, Confluence, Visio/Lucidchart
