## A scenario question, answered using my case study

Question: "How would you approach gathering requirements for a new
online checkout process?"

My approach: I'd start by mapping who's affected — customers, payment
provider, customer support — then look at existing data (e.g. where
users abandon checkout on the cinema site) before running any
interviews, so I'm not just guessing. I'd validate the seat-hold/expiry
issue with a short user survey at the drop-off point, then document
the fix as a specific, testable requirement (e.g. "seats release after
5 minutes") rather than a vague goal like "improve checkout."
