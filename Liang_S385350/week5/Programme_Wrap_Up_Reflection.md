# A Letter to the BA I'm Becoming

Writing this as a letter to myself in six months, rather than a formal
report — it's easier to be honest that way.

Dear future me,

**When I started, I thought the job was mostly:**
Writing documents and diagrams.

**I now think it's actually mostly:**
Asking the right questions early enough, and being willing to look at
the failure paths (like the seat-hold expiry case) instead of just the
happy path — the documents are just where that thinking gets recorded.

**The artefact I'm proudest of from these five stages is:**
The seat-hold user story rewrite — going from a vague, untestable line
to a specific, build-ready requirement with real acceptance criteria.

**The skill I still feel shaky on:**
Power BI's DAX formulas — I can build a chart from a clean table but
haven't practised writing real measures yet.

**One thing I'd tell someone starting this programme on day one:**
Pick one running example project early (mine was the cinema booking
site) — reusing it across every exercise made each new topic click
faster, since I already understood the domain.
