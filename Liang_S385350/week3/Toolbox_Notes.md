# Toolbox Notes — Diagramming and Workshops

## What I used this stage

| Tool | Best for | My honest take |
|------|----------|------------------|
| Lucidchart / Visio | Formal process diagrams | Good for the final swimlane diagram I'd hand to developers |
| Miro / Mural | Live workshops, sticky-note sessions | Better for brainstorming the "what could go wrong" list than for the final documentation |
| Confluence | Publishing the final documentation | Used to draft the seat-hold requirement page linking back to the diagram |

## A rule of thumb I landed on

Use a messy board (Miro) to think out loud with stakeholders, then move
the agreed version into a formal diagram (Lucidchart/Visio) once the
group has actually aligned — trying to make things look "final" too
early in a workshop tends to shut down useful disagreement.
